Sweet Spot — sound asset credits

The bundled fanfare must be a royalty-free or properly licensed sound. Until
you drop one in here, the app silently no-ops the in-app fanfare and falls
back to the system default for the lock-screen notification.

When you add a sound:

1. Convert to AAC-in-CAF, ≤30 seconds:
       afconvert -f caff -d aac in.wav fanfare.caf
2. Drop fanfare.caf into SweetSpot/Resources/ in Xcode (added to the
   SweetSpot target).
3. Record the source and license below.

---

| Asset       | Source URL              | License                 | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------------|-------------------------|-------|
| fanfare.caf | <add your source>       | <add license, e.g. CC0> |       |

Suggested royalty-free sources: Pixabay, Freesound (CC0 only),
incompetech.com (Creative Commons).
